===================================================================================================================
K. IMPORTANT INTERPRETATION
===================================================================================================================
FULLY USDA-MAPPED means every ingredient in the recipe has at least one USDA mapping.
It does NOT mean that every USDA mapping is semantically correct.

FULLY UNIQUE-MAPPED is stricter: every ingredient has exactly one USDA mapping.

STRICT CKD-NUTRIENT COMPLETE is stricter again: every ingredient has exactly one USDA mapping,
and that USDA record contains sodium, potassium, phosphorus, and protein.

These coverage statistics should therefore be reported separately from manually validated
semantic mapping accuracy.
